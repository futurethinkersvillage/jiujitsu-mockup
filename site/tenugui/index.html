Two arts, one root

柔道

Judo

“The gentle way.” Founded in Tokyo in 1882 by Jigorō Kanō. You learn to fall safely, to break someone's balance and to throw, then to pin. An Olympic sport since 1964.

柔術

Brazilian jiu jitsu

“The gentle art.” Judo master Mitsuyo Maeda taught it to the Gracie family in Brazil around 1917, and they built it into a complete ground game. Smaller people control bigger people with leverage.

Same root, 柔 (jū): yield to force, then redirect it. Training both makes you better at each.

The scrolls on our wall

Three brushed maxims from Ernst's dojo in Alberta. Judo's founder, Jigorō Kanō, built the art on the first two.

  • 精力善用

    Seiryoku zen'yō

    Maximum efficiency. Use your energy in the best possible way: technique over strength.

  • 自他共栄

    Jita kyōei

    Mutual welfare and benefit. You and your partner both get better, or neither of you does.

  • 柔能制剛

    Jū yoku gō o seisu

    Softness controls hardness. Give way to force, then redirect it.

Your first class

Nervous is normal. Here's exactly what happens.

  1. Send us a message

    Tell us who's coming and which class. We'll confirm the time and send the waiver.

  2. Arrive ten minutes early

    Sign the waiver, meet the coach, see where to change. Parents are welcome to stay and watch.

  3. Wear gym clothes

    T-shirt, shorts or track pants with no zips or buttons. Bring water and sandals for off the mat. No gi needed yet.

  4. Train

    Bow in, warm up, learn to fall, learn one technique, then try it with a partner at your own pace.

Questions

Do I need to be fit or have any experience?

No. Most people start out of shape and with no experience. You'll get fitter by training, and you'll always train at your own pace.

What's the difference between judo and jiu jitsu?

Judo is mostly standing: throws, then pins. Brazilian jiu jitsu is mostly on the ground: control and submissions. Jiu jitsu grew out of judo about a hundred years ago, so they fit together naturally.

Is it safe?

Everyone learns to fall before they learn to throw, and everyone learns to tap. Coaches supervise every round and pair people by size and experience.

How young can kids start?

Around five, depending on the child (TBC with the instructor). Kids classes are about coordination, confidence and respect first.

Do I need to buy a gi?

Not to start. After a few classes we'll help you choose one. A judo gi works for both arts.

Can I drop in while visiting Wells Gray?

Yes. Visitors, seasonal workers and crews in town for the summer are welcome. Message us before you come.
